3. How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device. Cookies allow us to recognize your preferences in order to tailor our pages and services to your specific needs. Some information is saved in these text files so that, when you visit our pages again, your browser or device is recognized and configured according to your previous preferences.

Cookies usually have an expiration date. Some cookies, for example, are automatically deleted when the browser is closed, while others may be stored on the device for a longer period until they are manually deleted.

We may use the following types of cookies under this Policy:

- Strictly necessary cookies, to ensure our pages or services function properly, such as authenticating logins. If you wish to continue accessing our pages or services, it will not be possible to refuse these types of cookies;
- Analytics cookies, to improve the content of our pages or services by providing information about how they are used and how often they are visited;
- Functionality cookies, to store information you previously provided, such as your language preferences, in order to enhance your browsing experience;
- Marketing cookies, to allow us to display advertisements that are more relevant to your interests, avoid showing you the same ads repeatedly, and optimize the delivery of our promotional content.

If you wish to refuse the installation of these cookies on your device or opt to remove cookies, you can do so through your browser settings. For more information on how to configure cookie usage in your browser, please refer to the website of your browser’s developer.

It is important to clarify that we are not responsible for the use of cookies by third parties. Cookies placed by third parties may continue to track your online activities, so we recommend regularly managing the cookies installed in your browser.
