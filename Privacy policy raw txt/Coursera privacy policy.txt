How We Use AI

We use certain AI product features ("Features") to achieve the purposes described above. We categorize these Features as follows:

Supporting Teaching and Course Generation: Features such as Course Builder, Program Builder, and Smart Ingestion take data from instructor content to generate course drafts that match Coursera for students to use.

Supporting Learning: These Features support learning, such as dubbing content into students' native languages (e.g., AI Dubbing) or helping students self-study (e.g., creating AI-generated flashcards) or providing feedback (e.g., coaching, coaching conversations, tool-based exercise generation, and role-playing).

Enabling Assessment and Review: These Features allow for Peer-to-Peer review, grade assessment, and providing written feedback (e.g., AI Enabled Peer Review, AI Open Ended Questions, and AI Integrity Check).
