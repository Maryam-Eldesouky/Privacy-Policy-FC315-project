2. Definitions

For the purposes of this policy, the following terms are used:

Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL): Saudi Arabia's legal framework for the collection, processing, and protection of personal data, ensuring privacy rights and compliance by organizations.
Personal Data: Any information relating to an identifiable individual, either directly or indirectly, such as a name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying details.
Processing: Any operation performed on personal data, including but not limited to collection, use, storage, modification, or disclosure of the data.
Data Subjects: Individuals whose personal data is collected, stored, or processed by an organization. This typically includes customers, employees, or other individuals interacting with the organization.
Anonymization: The process of irreversibly altering personal data so that an individual can no longer be identified, either directly or indirectly. Once data has been anonymized, it cannot be re-identified and is no longer considered personal data under the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

10. Data Subject Rights

At Jarir Marketing, we prioritize your privacy and ensure you can exercise your rights under the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), which include:

Right to Be Informed: To know how your data is collected, processed, and shared, as detailed in our Privacy Policy.
Right to Access: You can request access to your data and receive a copy.
Right to Rectification: If your data is inaccurate or incomplete, you can update your records through your account or you can submit a request, and we will promptly update our records.
Right to Erasure: You can request the deletion or anonymization of your data if it is no longer needed or legally required to be retained.
Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent at any time, and we will stop processing your data unless another legal basis applies.

12. Cookie Consent

Cookie consent refers to the permission you grant for our website to store or retrieve information on your device, primarily using cookies. This information enables us to understand your preferences, elevate your browsing experience, and provide services tailored specifically to you.

Upon your first visit to our website, you will be prompted to provide consent for the use of cookies, except for function cookies, which are essential for the website's seamless operation and do not require consent. You can customize your preferences for other categories of cookies, such as marketing, preferences, measurement, and social media cookies.

To manage or update your cookie preferences at any time, simply click on the icon located at the bottom left of the website or the application. This will provide you with access to our Cookie Settings page, where you can adjust your consent or revoke it for any non-essential cookies.
