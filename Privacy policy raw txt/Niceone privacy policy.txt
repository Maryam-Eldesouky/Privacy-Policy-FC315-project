Information Collected Through Your Visit to the Nice One Website

The Website servers automatically capture your Internet Protocol (IP) address when you visit the Website. (Your Internet Protocol address is the number assigned to the computer you use, which enables other devices connected to the Internet to identify the destination of the data transmitted from them and collect certain information such as browser type and search engine, without identifying your personal identity.) The Website's use of your Internet Protocol address helps diagnose problems occurring on its servers and also helps conduct the statistics necessary to measure Website usage (the number of visitors and the language of the computer you use). The Website does not permit any party outside its technical team to access your Internet Protocol address.

When you visit one of the pages on our Website that contains a social media plugin, your browser will establish a direct connection with the servers of such social media plugins, such as Google and Facebook. These plugins transfer their content directly to your browser, which in turn incorporates such information into the Website, enabling the receipt of information relating to your access to the selected page on our Website. For this reason, data relating to your visit to the Nice One Website is transferred. Such data certainly does not include personal information such as your name or telephone number. If you do not wish to share data relating to your visit to the Nice One Website, you may disable tracking through your browser settings.

The Website also uses Google Analytics, a service provided by Google to analyze Website pages and understand how users interact with them. Google Analytics relies on cookies, and the information generated by cookies regarding your use of the Website may be transferred to Google's servers. Google uses this information to evaluate Website usage, prepare reports for Website operators, and provide other services related to Internet activity. This information may be shared with third parties if required by law or if a third party processes the data on behalf of Google.

You may refuse the use of cookies through your browser settings; however, please note that this may affect certain Website functions and prevent you from fully benefiting from the Website. By using this Website, you agree to the processing of your data by Google for the purposes described above, provided that the processed data does not include any personal information and is limited only to data relating to your visit and activity on the Website.
