Why Does the Company Use Cookies?

When you use our website or applications, we place cookies or similar technologies on your device to better understand your interaction with the Platform. You can always access detailed information about cookies and make changes to cookie usage via the "Cookie Preferences" button located at the bottom of the homepage on the www.trendyol.com website. Please note that the use of mandatory cookies is essential for the operation of our website, and therefore, you do not have the option to manage this type of cookie. When you interact with our advertisements on third party platforms we obtain information about that interaction. This might include whether you clicked on the ad, or how long the ad was shown before an interaction took place, if by viewing that advertisement you directly or at a later time purchased a good on our platform, and indirectly the effectiveness of those advertisements.

In the event you visit us on social media platforms we are the data controller of all the information you place on those pages or when you communicate with us. When you visit Fan Pages, social media platforms collect information about your use of those pages through the use of cookies and other method, which allows Trendyol to view certain statistics about the use of our pages so called "page insights". For the collection and use of these data the social media platform and Trendyol are considered jointly responsible. For more information on how the social media platforms use your personal data please visit their website.
