Your Rights Under PDPL and How to Exercise Them
As a Mobily customer, You have the following rights:

- Right to be Informed: You must be informed about the reasons for collecting Your personal data, the identity of the data controller, and any potential risks of not providing the requested data.
- Right of Access: You can request access to Your personal data.
- Right to Correction: You may request corrections or updates to inaccurate or incomplete personal data held by the Us
- Right to Erasure: You can request the deletion of your personal data if it is no longer necessary for the original purpose or if You withdraw consent.
- Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw previously given consent at any time, except where the law mandates otherwise.
- Right to Obtain Personal Data: You can request a copy of your personal data in a readable format to understand what is being held about You.
- Right to File a Complaint: You have the right to submit a complaint to the relevant regulatory authority if You believe their personal data has been misused or their data privacy rights have been violated, in accordance with applicable laws.

Mobily is committed to informing you of your rights and has established dedicated channels to help you exercise them. We aim to respond to all data protection–related requests or complaints within 30 days and maintain records of all submissions in accordance with PDPL requirements

Mobily Website Usage of Cookies
We use cookies and similar technologies to ensure website functionality, enhance Your experience, and continuously improve our services

We use cookies to:
- Protect users from fraud and strengthen website and network security.
- Analyze how visitors use our website and digital platforms to improve usability and performance.
- Understand customer interests and identify products, services, or offers that may be most relevant.
- Deliver personalized content and marketing across Mobily’s website, apps, and social media platforms.
- Ensure that personal data collected through cookies is processed only for the purposes for which it was originally collected.

1: Personal Data Collected Through Cookies
We may collect the following information when you visit our website:
- Device and equipment details, browsing patterns, and interaction data.
- Technical details such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and connection information.
- Website usage information, including visit duration, pages accessed, navigation paths, and time of requests.
- Location data (where enabled) to optimize service functionality.

This information helps Mobily understand how visitors interact with its website, allowing for improved security, functionality, and customer experience

2: Categories of Cookies Used on Mobily’s Website
Our website uses the following categories of cookies:
- Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for website functionality, enabling secure login, navigation, and use of key site features.
- Performance Cookies: Collect anonymous data on how visitors use the website to help improve performance and usability.
- Functionality Cookies: To remember user preferences (such as language, region, or login details) to provide a more personalized experience.
- Marketing and Third-Party Cookies: Used by Us and our trusted partners (such as Google Analytics and Facebook Pixel) to analyze site traffic, enhance digital marketing, and measure campaign effectiveness.

We maintain the highest standards of confidentiality and ensure that data collected through cookies is handled responsibly and securely.

3: How to Enable or Disable Cookies
By continuing to browse or use this website, you explicitly consent to the placement and use of cookies and to the collection, use, and transfer, whether in electronic or other form, of your personal data in accordance with the Mobily Privacy Notice.

If you do not wish to accept cookies, you may adjust your browser settings to block or delete them. The links below provide instructions for the most used browsers:
Google Chrome – (chrome://settings/clearBrowserData)
Microsoft Edge – (edge://settings/clearbrowserdata)
Mozilla Firefox – (https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/delete-browsing-search-download-history-firefox)
Opera – (https://help.opera.com/en/latest/web-preferences/#cookies)

4: What Happens if You Disable Cookies?
If you disable cookies, certain features of Our website may not function properly. You may experience limited access to secure areas, slower page loading, or loss of stored preferences such as language or login details. Disabling cookies may also reduce personalization and affect your ability to complete transactions or interact with specific website features.

While You can manage cookies to enhance privacy, doing so may impact the full functionality and convenience of your browsing experience on Our website.
