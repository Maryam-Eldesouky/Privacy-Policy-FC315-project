Cookies and Similar Technologies, Pixel Tags, and Other Identifiers

Cookies are small data files typically stored on your device when you visit websites and online services. The text in a cookie contains a string of numbers and letters that can uniquely identify a device, and may contain other information. This allows a web server to recognize your browser each time it connects.

We use other technologies, such as browser storage and plugins (e.g., HTML5, IndexedDB, and WebSQL). Similar to cookies, other such technologies may store small amounts of data on a device. Pixel tags are often used in conjunction with cookies. In many cases, refusing to use cookies will impair the effectiveness of pixel tags associated with such cookies.

We may also create, use, and disclose privacy-protective identifiers, such as hashed identifiers. As described above, hashing is the conversion of original contact information (e.g., email address or phone number) into a corresponding value (usually a long string of alphanumeric characters) that itself cannot reveal your identity or contact information.

If you use the Netflix App (e.g., the main Netflix App or Netflix Game App) on a mobile device, tablet, or streaming device, we may collect resettable device identifiers from the device. Resettable device identifiers are similar to cookies and can be found on many mobile devices and tablets (e.g., "Advertising Identifiers" on Apple iOS devices and "Google Advertising IDs" on Android devices) and certain streaming devices.

Why Does Netflix Use These Technologies?

We use these various technologies for a variety of reasons, including providing our service (e.g., making access to our service easier by remembering you when you return); managing and operating our business; researching, analyzing, and improving our service (e.g., improving website performance, monitoring visitor traffic and operations on our website, and testing the effectiveness of our user interfaces); sending marketing and informational messages (e.g., providing and tailoring our marketing, and understanding how people interact with our emails, marketing content, and marketing on third-party services); supporting advertising; providing security, safety, and fraud prevention; complying with laws and enforcing the Netflix Terms of Use. These technologies enable Netflix, Netflix Marketing Providers, and Advertising Companies to collect personal information (e.g., pages you visit and device and network information) when you use our service, and to collect information about your online activities across different websites.

We use pixel tags in emails to understand how members interact with our service. We use pixel tags to understand when links in messages are clicked or emails are opened. We may also use pixel tags on advertisers' websites and apps to support ad delivery.

Netflix uses hashed identifiers and resettable identifiers to display marketing information about Netflix content on our service or third-party services, and to support advertising (including serving ads to you) as well as for analysis and optimization.
