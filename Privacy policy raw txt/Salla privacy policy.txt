Integration with Google Services (Google Shopping API):

As part of Salla Platform's commitment to providing comprehensive services to merchants, practitioners, and consumers, the Integration with Google Services feature has been enabled, specifically with the Google Merchant Center platform, aimed at facilitating product synchronization and securely and systematically managing store data.
1. Granting Permission and Access to Data
When the merchant grants explicit permission through Google's authorized authentication mechanism, Salla Platform accesses specific data from the merchant's Google account, including but not limited to:

Product and inventory data registered in Google Shopping (such as product IDs, prices, descriptions, quantities).

Relevant Google Merchant Center account settings.

2. Purpose of Data Processing
This data is used solely for enabling technical integration between the merchant's store on Salla Platform and their Google Shopping account, including:

Creating, updating, or synchronizing product listings.

Managing connection and authentication between the two systems.

This is done to ensure catalog data consistency between both platforms and enhance the online store's performance.

3. Data Protection and Regulatory Compliance
Salla Platform affirms the following:

Google user data is used only for the purposes specified above, serving the merchant's legitimate interest in improving their online store experience.

Salla Platform does not sell, lease, or transfer this data to any third party for marketing, commercial, or advertising purposes outside the scope of the service provided.

Data is retained for the period necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, in accordance with the General Privacy Policy in force on Salla Platform, or deleted upon the user's request, upon integration cancellation, or upon app removal.

Salla Platform complies with all provisions of the Personal Data Protection Law in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its Implementing Regulations, including applying the necessary security, confidentiality, and privacy standards to protect data when integrating with any external services.

Integration with AI Assistants (Salla AI Connector):

A merchant can connect their store to an AI assistant, such as ChatGPT by OpenAI or Claude by Anthropic, through the Salla AI connector. The merchant chooses to connect, signs in with their Salla account, approves the permissions the assistant receives, and can disconnect at any time from the AI assistant's connector settings.

Granting Permission and Access to Data. Once connected, the assistant can read the store information the merchant authorizes, including products, orders, customers, shipments and reports. It can also make changes the merchant asks for, such as updating a product price. The AI assistant asks the merchant to approve changes before they are made.

Information Shared with the Assistant. When the merchant asks about a specific order, customer or cart, the information sent to the assistant can include the consumer's personal information described in this policy, such as name, contact details, delivery address and map location. For a bank-transfer order it can include the transfer receipt and the store's receiving bank account, masked to its last four digits. Lists and reports generally show consumers' names and city, with contact details only when the merchant asks for them. Date of birth, gender and last sign-in date are sent only when the merchant asks for them.

Information Never Shared. Salla never sends passwords, access tokens or other credentials, full bank account or IBAN numbers, consumers' IP addresses or device details, or redeemable digital codes. Private links that would let someone open a consumer's order, checkout or downloads are not sent, except an abandoned cart's recovery link and an order's own invoice and shipping label, which are sent only when the merchant opens that cart or order.

Purpose, Recipients and Location. The AI provider receives this information only to answer the merchant's request, and handles it under the merchant's own agreement with that provider. The AI provider, and the infrastructure that runs the connector, may process it outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any transfer or disclosure of personal data outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia shall be carried out in accordance with the applicable requirements of the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, its Implementing Regulations, and the regulations governing the transfer of personal data outside the Kingdom.

Retention. The connector keeps pending change previews for up to 15 minutes, keeps cached report results for up to 90 days, and keeps records of connector activity, including the changes made, for up to 12 months for security, troubleshooting, audit and resolving disputes about those changes. Information the AI provider receives is kept according to the merchant's agreement with that provider.

Responsibility. The merchant decides what to share through an AI assistant and with which provider. Merchants are responsible for using consumer information obtained this way in line with their own obligations to their customers.
