What is the Privacy Policy and what does it cover?

Effective October 16, 2026 | View printable version | View previous versions

Section Highlights
Meta wants you to understand what information we collect, and how and why we use and share it. That's why we encourage you to read our Privacy Policy. It helps you use Meta Products in the way that's right for you.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, retain and transfer information. This policy also explains your rights. Each section of this policy includes useful examples and uses plain language to help you more easily understand our data practices. We also provide links to resources where you can learn more about the privacy topics that interest you.

It's important that you know how to control your privacy settings, so we also explain where you can manage your information in the settings of the Meta Products you use. You can update these settings to shape your experience.

How long do we retain your information?

Section Highlights
We retain information if it is necessary for us to provide our products, comply with legal obligations or protect our or others' rights and interests. We decide how long we need information on a case-by-case basis. Here are the factors we consider when deciding how long to keep information:
- If we need the information to operate or provide our products. For example, we need to keep some of your information to maintain your account. Learn more.
- The feature we use the information for and how that feature works. For example, messages sent using Messenger's vanish mode are retained for less time than regular messages. Learn more.
- How long we need to retain the information to comply with certain legal obligations. See some examples.
- If we need it for other legitimate purposes, such as to prevent harm, investigate possible violations of our terms or policies, promote safety and integrity, or protect ourselves, including our rights, property or products.

In some instances, and for specific reasons, we keep information for longer. Read our policy to learn about the circumstances in which we may retain your information.
